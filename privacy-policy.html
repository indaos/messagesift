MessageSift Privacy Policy

Effective: 22 November 2026

MessageSift reads what comes in on your Mac, such as mail, messages and work tools, and shows only the items that need you. It is built so that your messages never leave your Mac.

Data we collect

None. MessageSift has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not send your mail, messages, contacts, calendar or any other personal information to us.

Mail and Messages

MessageSift reads Mail and Messages only from the folders you choose yourself (your Mail and Messages folders in your Library). It reads them without changing anything. You can remove this access at any time in Settings › General › Folders › Forget Access.

Documents you add

Scans, PDFs and images you add (from your iPhone camera, with File › Add Document or by dragging them onto the window) are copied into the app's storage on your Mac, and their text is recognized on your Mac.

Understanding your messages

Sorting, labels and summaries are made on your Mac with rules and Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence). No message text is sent to any AI service. What MessageSift learns from you (for example when you mark something as not important) is stored only on your Mac.

Contacts, calendars and reminders

If you allow it, MessageSift uses Contacts to show names instead of phone numbers, Calendars to show meeting invitations waiting for your answer, and Reminders to show open reminders with a due date. If you send an item to Reminders or Calendar, MessageSift adds it there on your Mac. If you send an item to Things, MessageSift hands it to the Things app on your Mac.

Work tools you connect

If you connect Slack, GitHub, GitLab, Jira, Linear, Microsoft (Teams and Outlook) or Todoist, MessageSift talks directly from your Mac to that service with the key or sign-in you provide. Keys are stored in your Mac's keychain. Data goes only between your Mac and that service; that service's own privacy policy applies to it. We never receive it.

The optional Connector

Mac notifications and WhatsApp can only be added with the separate, free MessageSift Connector, which you can install yourself. It runs on your Mac, has no network access, and passes what it reads to MessageSift through a file in the app's private shared folder on your Mac.

Where your data is stored

Everything MessageSift keeps (actions, what is done, settings and what it learned) is stored in the app's private storage on your Mac, and in a shared folder that only its widget and Mail extension can open. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
