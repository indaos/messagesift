MessageSift Support

Need help? Email support@daos.cloud and we'll reply within two working days.

How do I add Mail and Messages?

When MessageSift asks, choose your Mail folder and then your Messages folder in the window that opens (both are in your Library folder). MessageSift only reads them. To start over, open Settings › General › Folders, click Forget Access, then choose the folders again.

Why don't I see an item I expected?

MessageSift shows only items that need an action or that you shouldn't miss. Ordinary messages and newsletters stay out of the way. Right-click an item and choose "This is a…" to teach MessageSift what something is. To see more, choose a longer time in Settings › General › Show last.

How do I add the widget?

Right-click your desktop, choose Edit Widgets, find MessageSift and drag a widget onto the desktop: Now (what needs you today), Spending, Parcels, Next trip or Insights. If the Done button doesn't react, turn off dimmed or monochrome widgets in System Settings › Desktop & Dock › Widgets.

How do I turn on the Mail extension?

In Mail, open Settings › Extensions and tick "MessageSift Urgency". It flags urgent mail inside Mail.

How do I connect Slack or a work tool?

Open Settings › Sources, choose the service and paste your key there. Each guide in the app tells you where to find the key.

Can MessageSift read Mac notifications or WhatsApp?

Not by itself: App Store apps can't read them. The free, open-source MessageSift Connector adds them. MessageSift works fully without it.

Is my mail uploaded?

No. Your mail and messages stay on your Mac and are understood with Apple's on-device model. See the privacy policy.
